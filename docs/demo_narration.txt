PromoPilot is an autonomous promotion planner for an electronics retailer. The problem is simple to describe but difficult to optimize. A promotion must balance inventory, competitor prices, customer behavior, demand events, and margin constraints.

PromoPilot combines five business signals: inventory, competitor pricing, demand events, customer segments, and product relationships. Instead of allowing a language model to guess the numbers, trusted Python calculations handle the arithmetic and hard constraints.

First, the system ingests the retail signals. Our synthetic MVP contains fifty products, seven customer segments, five demand events, seven hundred and fifty competitor observations, and product relationships.

Next, the planner ranks promotion opportunities. High inventory, aging stock, competitor pressure, and viable margin increase the opportunity score.

The planner then selects a candidate strategy. For the selected product, it chooses a promotion mechanism, discount, duration, region, and target customer segment. The decision is accompanied by reasons such as excess inventory, aging inventory, and competitor pressure.

Before launch, the planner simulates the promotion. It tests candidate discount levels and estimates fourteen-day units, revenue, and margin. A candidate is not approved simply because it sounds attractive.

The constraint validator checks hard business rules, including minimum margin and marketing budget. If a candidate fails, the agentic workflow rejects it and evaluates another candidate. This gives us a measurable re-planning loop rather than a simple prompt and response.

The final output is an explainable promotion plan with the selected product, discount, duration, target segment, expected outcome, and constraint status.

PromoPilot demonstrates nine retail capabilities: product selection, promotion mechanism, discount optimization, cannibalization awareness, product relationships, inventory constraints, geographic customization, competitor awareness, and pre-launch simulation.

The MVP can be extended with live retail data, learned demand elasticity, richer cannibalization models, and a human approval workflow. The key idea is to make promotion planning faster, safer, and explainable.
